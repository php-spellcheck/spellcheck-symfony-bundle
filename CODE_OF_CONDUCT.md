# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Expected: be respectful of differing opinions and experience, give and accept
constructive feedback, take responsibility for mistakes, and focus on what is
best for the project and its users.

Not acceptable: sexualised language or imagery, trolling, insulting or
derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or private
harassment, publishing others' private information without permission, and any
other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests,
discussions and commits — and when an individual represents the project in
public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to <raffaele.carelle@gmail.com>. Reports are
reviewed privately and promptly. Maintainers may warn, remove content,
temporarily ban or permanently ban any contributor whose behaviour they
consider a violation, and they are obliged to respect the privacy and security
of the reporter.
